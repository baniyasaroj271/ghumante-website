              A practical note

              Travel safely around Kathmandu Valley.

              • Keep valuables secure. Use a zipped day bag, especially on crowded buses, markets, and busy squares.
              • Confirm the bus destination. Ask the conductor before boarding and check the vehicle sign; routes and stops can vary.
              • Cross roads carefully. Traffic may approach from several directions. Use marked crossings where available and wait for a clear gap.
              • Respect religious sites. Dress modestly, walk clockwise where customary, remove shoes when asked, and ask before photographing people or ceremonies.
              • Keep emergency details accessible. Save your accommodation address, passport copy, travel insurance details, and the numbers above offline.

              Three cities. Thousands of small detours.
